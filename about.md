# Daily Demon Suggestions

Tap the green star button in the main menu. Pick what you want to beat:

- **Easy / Medium / Hard / Insane** demons come from the **GDDL** (GD Demon Ladder).
- **Extreme** demons come from the **AREDL**.
- **List** demons come from the **Pointercrate** Demon List.

Each category suggests up to 10 levels (with at least 4000 likes by default). Tap **Pick** on the one you want to beat today, or **View** to open it.

Easy to Hard refresh daily, Insane and Extreme weekly, List monthly. You can change every one of those in the mod settings, along with the like minimum, the tier ranges, and more.

The first time you open it, a short setup asks for your hardest demon, favourite gamemodes, level style and skills so suggestions fit you. Use **My Profile** to redo it.
